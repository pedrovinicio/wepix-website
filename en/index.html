Frequently asked questions

Get answers about WePix

When is WePix useful?

WePix can be used in many situations, such as:

  • Group trips
  • Dinners and nights out with friends
  • Shared household expenses
  • Events or parties
  • Any situation where several people split costs
Does WePix process payments?

No. WePix organizes and calculates the amounts each person should pay or receive. Payments can be made using the method the group prefers (transfer, PIX, cash, etc.).

How does expense splitting work?

You add an expense, choose who was involved, and the app automatically calculates how much each person owes. You can also customize amounts if someone paid more or less.

Can I split expenses unevenly?

Yes! You can split expenses equally or set different amounts for each person.

Does the app show who still needs to pay?

Yes. WePix keeps an updated summary showing:

  • Who owes
  • Who has amounts to receive
  • Overall group status
Do I need to create an account to use it?

Yes. Creating an account allows you to sync data, share groups and keep your expenses secure.

Is my data secure?

Yes. WePix uses security best practices to protect user and group information.

Can I use the app on international trips?

Yes! The app works normally to organize expenses anywhere in the world.

Do I need to be online to use it?

Some features may work offline, but internet is required to sync data with other group members.

Is WePix free?

WePix is 100% free, with no ads whatsoever.

What devices is WePix available on?

Android and iOS.
