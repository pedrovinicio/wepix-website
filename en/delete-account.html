In compliance with LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law)

What happens when you delete your account?

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account data: Name, email, password and profile photo
  • Profile information: PIX key and preferences
  • Messages: All messages you sent in groups
  • Expenses: Expenses you created (group history will be kept for other participants)
  • Group membership: You will be removed from all groups

Important information

  • Deletion is permanent and irreversible
  • Some data may be retained for legal obligations under Brazilian law

How to request deletion

To request account deletion, send an email to:

privacy@wepix-app.com

Include the email address associated with your WePix account in the email.
