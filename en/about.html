About WePix

What is WePix?

WePix is a mobile app that makes splitting expenses between friends easy. Perfect for group trips, shared houses, dinners and any situation where you need to divide bills fairly and without hassle.

Main features

  • Expense tracking: Add expenses in seconds and split equally or customize amounts
  • Built-in chat: Communicate with your group in one place
  • PIX payments: Settle debts instantly by copying payment details
  • Unlimited groups: Create as many groups as you need for different occasions
